## Built-in imagegen concept prompt

+Create a final production-quality sheet of eight ORIGINAL upgrade icons for Lost Warden, a top-down retro sci-fi survival shmup. Reference 1 is the current tiny icon atlas: retain meanings of its FIRST EIGHT symbols ONLY, but redraw them with much richer, more distinctive hardware detail. Reference 2 is actual gameplay: match its abandoned orbital fortress, battered angular grey fighter, dark industrial metal, rust/aged bronze, restrained hot emitters and shadowy space atmosphere. These are HIGH-DEFINITION MENU ICONS, not enlarged 16px pictograms. Art direction: carefully clustered detailed hand-pixelled 2D videogame equipment art, bevelled gunmetal edges, layered worn armor plating, recessed bolts, tiny amber status lights, weathered bronze fittings, crisp hard pixels; not generic glossy mobile game badges, not flat emojis, no circular backplates, no text, no labels, no numbers, no baked UI frames. Shape clarity before microdetail. Consistent upper-left light, transparent background, no fog, no blurry glow or antialiasing. Draw an orderly 4 columns by 2 rows atlas, equal square cells, one wholly contained centered icon per cell with generous transparent gutters, no overlap. Each icon uses roughly a 96x96 logical pixel design in a 128x128 cell, clearly readable displayed at 64x64 and still distinct at32x32. Reading order: (1) SPEED paired armored thruster modules with two luminous downward orange exhausts, tips upward; (2) DAMAGE heavy axial pulse cannon emitter pointing UP, a powerful ivory/hot-gold core and red/bronze armored shoulders; (3) FIRE RATE three distinct staggered upward pulse rounds emerging from a compact dark repeating-fire mechanism, much narrower rounds than damage icon; (4) XP ATTRACTION angular industrial horseshoe electromagnet, worn steel and bronze casing, restrained cyan energized tips holding a floating small GOLD diamond in its opening; (5) SIDE SHOTS: battered symmetrical top-down grey Nomad fighter NOSE POINTING UP, recognizable angular wings, EXACTLY TWO separated short ivory/orange projectiles travelling UP, one well above each wing. Engines behind the ship emit DOWN. Nose and front projectiles must never point down; (6) REPAIR battered metallic hull repair module with a clearly legible recessed green cross and tiny rivets, no white stock medical badge; (7) SHIELD concave cyan deflector arc over a dark angular projector mechanism, not a medieval shield, no duplicate horseshoe magnet silhouette; (8) SALVAGE a compact stack of battered bronze/gold ship plates and metal ingots with distinctive staggered silhouette and tiny rivet scars. ## Final native treatment

Use original 32px pixel clusters, steel and aged bronze mechanisms, navy shadows, ivory highlights, tiny status lights and clear energy accents. Keep upward weapon orientation and distinctive silhouettes: dual engines, axial cannon, triple cadence mechanism, horseshoe magnet with XP diamond, forward Nomad with paired shots, repair module, concave field projector and staggered salvage plates. Do not reproduce high-resolution surface noise or soft lighting from the reference. Retain native cell margins and integer display scaling.
