# Lost Warden — asset and creative-content licensing

This document describes the project's licensing policy going forward. It does **not** revoke permissions already granted in previously published versions, including any assets historically released under the MIT License.

## Source code versus creative assets

The [MIT License](LICENSE.txt) is retained for project source code (Godot scripts and source-code components). It should not be interpreted as a blanket MIT grant for **newly added** original art, music, sound effects, written lore, original characters or branding after this policy is adopted.

For newly contributed original creative assets whose copyright is controlled by the project owner, **all rights are reserved unless an asset-specific license or written permission says otherwise**. No general permission is granted to reuse such assets in another game or redistribute them commercially. Permission to run or share official Lost Warden builds containing those assets is limited to the distribution permissions expressly provided with those builds.

## Existing assets and third-party material

The current `assets/` tree contains legacy game-jam content, references, fonts and potentially third-party material. **Do not assume that every existing file is owned by the project or covered by this all-rights-reserved policy.** Before assigning a specific license to existing resources, establish authorship, source, historical license grants and permission to redistribute.

- `assets/fonts/`: respect each font's own license; document it before redistribution.
- `assets/audio/`, `assets/sprites/`, `assets/art/`, `assets/ui/`, `assets/sources/`, `assets/palettes/`: provenance and licensing must be checked on an asset-by-asset basis.
- Referenced trademarks and third-party artwork are not transferred or relicensed by this document.

## AI-assisted or AI-generated material

Some materials are AI-assisted or AI-generated. Their copyright status can depend on applicable law and the extent of human creative input. The policy above asserts rights **only where rights actually exist and are held by the relevant author**. Check the generation service's terms and keep creation/provenance records. AI-generated material is not automatically proprietary merely because this notice says so.

## Contributions

Do not submit new art, music, sound effects or narrative assets without first agreeing on the applicable rights and license with maintainers. Source-code contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Requests for permission

Please open a GitHub issue in this repository to discuss reuse of original creative material or a commercial license. Permission requests do not change the MIT terms of covered code or earlier valid grants.
