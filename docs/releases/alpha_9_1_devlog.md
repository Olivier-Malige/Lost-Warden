# Alpha 9.1 — Wave counter and background fixes

Alpha 9.1 is a small bug-fix update, now available in the browser and as Windows and Linux downloads.

Thanks to everyone playing and sharing feedback! A special thanks to swoopie for reaching Wave 18 with a score of 14,947 and reporting the counter mismatch.

What changed:

- Wave numbers now continue past 18 during endless play.
- The game-over screen immediately includes your latest run when showing your highest wave and high score.
- The red nebula keeps its intended transparency.
- Background elements no longer flicker on the right during camera movement on wide screens.
- Nebula textures have smoother horizontal joins, with cleaner debris edges.

Existing saves remain compatible. Power-up balance is unchanged in this patch; the larger progression and wave-generation rework is still planned for future updates.

Thanks again for helping improve Lost Warden — and keep those scores coming!
