# Rejected defensive role icon prompts

Historical prompts for a visual direction rejected by the maintainer on 2026-10-26. The built-in image generation tool produced a mirror and starburst that did not fit the game's native pixel art. These standalone runtime assets were removed; the prompts below document the rejected experiment.

Current artwork is authored directly in [upgrade_icons_64.aseprite](upgrade_icons_64.aseprite) and [upgrade_miniatures.aseprite](upgrade_miniatures.aseprite). The maintainer subsequently requested the original Reflection icons and owner colors again: column 6 now matches the original red/blue artwork exactly, on `Original team reflector sphere`. The `Golden retaliation` layer retains column 18's native ship and the golden frame from [impact_explosion.aseprite](../combat/impact_explosion.aseprite). The runtime pulse uses its dedicated [native-size golden animation](../player/weapons/retaliation_pulse.aseprite), refined after the maintainer rejected magnified effect pixels. It no longer draws the rejected pointed vector burst.

## Reflection

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a SINGLE transparent-background pixel-art game upgrade icon, to be downsampled to 64x64 and 16x16 for a retro space shooter. The recent sprite atlas is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: crisp sparse pixel clusters, navy outlines, cool metallic highlights. Generate just ONE isolated icon centered on transparent canvas, no atlas and no text. Subject: a small tilted SILVER-CYAN MIRROR PLATE at the right; a clearly visible red-orange projectile streak approaches the mirror diagonally from the upper left and a bright CYAN ARROW ricochets back toward the lower left. The incoming and outgoing directions must form an unmistakable V bounce at the mirror. Simple chunky silhouette, 6 to 8 large pixel clusters, bold arrowhead; subtle beveled silver mirror edge, pale icy cyan shine. No spaceship, NO CIRCLE, NO BUBBLE, no shield emblem, no starburst, no radial wave, no border, no labels. Plenty of transparent padding. Pixel-art rendering, hard edges, no blur or smooth gradients. This must read as REFLECT ENEMY PROJECTILES rather than contact protection.

## Retaliation

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a SINGLE transparent-background pixel-art game upgrade icon, to be downsampled to 64x64 and 16x16 for a retro space shooter. The recent sprite atlas is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: crisp sparse pixel clusters and navy outlines. Generate just ONE isolated icon centered on transparent canvas, no atlas and no text. Subject: an unmistakable warm AMBER-ORANGE COUNTERATTACK STARBURST caused by a close impact: a compact bright ivory impact core with eight broad pointed orange-gold energy spikes radiating outward, dark russet bevel shading, three or four small separated orange shards. Bold symmetric spiky silhouette, chunky low-detail pixel clusters, visually explosive. No spaceship, NO CIRCLE, NO BUBBLE, no mirror, no arrow, no shield emblem, no continuous perimeter, no border, no labels. Plenty of transparent padding. Hard pixel-art edges, no blur or smooth gradients. This must read as CONTACT COUNTERATTACK and look completely different from a cyan mirror with a reflected projectile.
